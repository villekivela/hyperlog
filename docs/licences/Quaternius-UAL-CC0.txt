Quaternius Universal Animation Library: the mannequin
=====================================================

The mannequin in the exercise prescription sheet is the Mannequin model from
the Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 asks for no credit. Hyperlog gives it anyway.

Hyperlog's own clips are baked from this model by tools/mannequins/. The
baked .glb files are Hyperlog's work, with the Quaternius mesh and rig inside.
