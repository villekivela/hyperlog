# The coach is words first, with no built-in rules

The Coach screen used to offer five situations (time off, pain, no gym, short on time, poor
recovery) with limit chips, and Claude as a sixth choice. The owner found it hard to read: the
limits repeated the situations, rows showed when they couldn't change anything, and the rules
couldn't see what the program already did. Sore hands in Ramp-in got +1 RIR on top of Ramp-in's
own +1 RIR, when only the grip swaps were needed.

So the coach is now your words to the Claude app, the way ADR 0001 hands off, and the rules are
gone. Claude reads the program, the week you're in and your sessions, so it can tell what a
situation needs. It can answer in words alone, or reply with a Change or a workout. The phone
always has signal where the owner trains, which was the rules' only advantage.

This replaces ADR 0001's "Offline rules cover the common situations without any model". The rest
of ADR 0001 stands: no model calls from the app, and an MCP connector waits until syncing is wanted.

## Considered options

- **Keep the rules as one-tap shortcuts beside the words**: instant and offline, but two ways in,
  and the rules' blind spot (they don't know the block) stays.
- **Keep the rules and redesign their screen**: tidier, same blind spot.
