Prototype: traced squat. Motion source switches the Barbell Back Squat between the authored pose and one rep traced from its technique clip with MediaPipe (shin, thigh and trunk angles, smoothed), retargeted onto the mannequin with the feet, hands and bar-over-mid-foot kept by IK. Other exercises stay authored. A figure in the exercise sheet, posed by code. Mannequin is the jointed mannequin from Quaternius's Universal Animation Library; Superhero is the base character from his Universal Base Characters (both CC0); Mannequiny is GDQuest's mannequin (CC BY 4.0). All are stripped of textures. The machines (Pulldown, Pressdown, Leg press, Chest press) are built from boxes and rods in code, like the barbell and bench; their weight stacks and sleds move with the rep. Drag the figure to turn it; double-tap to reset. The bar at the bottom switches style.
