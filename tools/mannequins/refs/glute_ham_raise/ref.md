# Glute-ham developer (`glute_ham_raise`)

## Movement and exercises served
Body prone-to-vertical on a hip pad with ankles locked under rollers and feet on a foot plate; knees fixed, flex the knees and extend the hips to raise the torso. Serves `glute_ham_raise`. No moving machine part: the user is the only moving body.

## Motion
- Machine is static; no moving bodies. The user's hinge axis sits at the knee (knees fixed in the rollers) and the hip (pad edge). Body ROM is human: about 90 degrees of hip and knee flexion.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Roller assembly slides along a rail in 1" steps, locked by two threaded knobs and a pop pin.
- Ankle pads adjust horizontally; no numeric range.
- Fit rule (general GHD practice, not from the page): hip pad edge at the crease of the hips so the torso can hinge freely, knees clear of the pad.
