# Trap bar (`trap_bar`)

## Movement and exercises served
A hexagonal frame the lifter stands inside, gripping two parallel handles at the sides. Serves `trap_bar_deadlift`. The bar is a rigid body; its handles are flipped by hand between a low and a high position and then fixed.

## Motion
Nothing moves while lifting. The dual-height handle is a rigid pair of rollers on a flip bracket (a fixed choice between two positions, not a joint during the lift): low position is the single-handle height, the flipped position raises the grip 102 mm. The lifter stands with the mid-line of the feet between the handles; with 450 mm plates the low handle centre (225 mm) is level with the plate centre.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Choose low (225 mm) or high (327 mm) handle height before the set, never both at once.
- Handles stay parallel and 635 mm apart; the lifter's hands grip the handle centre.
- Loaded plates rest on the floor; the frame sits on the plates, so handle heights above assume 450 mm plates (inferred from the 8.875 in value matching a 450 mm plate radius).
- Rackable length only matters if the bar is placed in a rack (not for the deadlift).
