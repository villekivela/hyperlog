# Safety squat bar (`safety_bar`)

## Movement and exercises served
A cambered bar with a padded yoke on the shoulders and two forward handles that hang in front of the lifter. Serves `safety_bar_squat` and `good_morning_barbell_safety_bar`. The bar is a rigid body; the lifter holds the forward handles or the yoke.

## Motion
Nothing moves. The 140 mm camber lowers the plate centreline below the shaft, so the load sits lower and forward. The handles angle forward and down from the yoke: the lifter's forearms rest on or grip them in front of the chest. No pivots on the bar itself (the Rogue page says fully welded).

## Adjustments and fit rules
- No adjustments; one bar fits all heights.
- The yoke sits on the trapezius and front deltoids, the handles are held loosely; the lifter stays upright (safety_bar_squat) or hinges forward at the hips (good_morning_barbell_safety_bar) with the handle pair in front of the torso.
- In the rack, hooks catch the straight shaft, not the yoke.
- Plates load on the sleeve end: expect the plate centre 140 mm below the shaft centre line.
