# Smith machine (`smith_machine`)

A barbell on linear guide rods between two uprights with rotating hooks. The bar can only travel along the rod axis.

## Movement and exercises served
Barbell moves along a fixed path (about 7 degrees off vertical on the angled model, vertical on the "Vertical Smith"). Hooks twist to rack or unrack at any of the bar-catch positions; safety stops catch the bar below the working range. Used for squat, bench, incline, seated press, lunge, split squat, RDL and JM press with a bench, step or plates placed under the bar.

Ids: ffe_smith_machine_lunge, smith_machine_bench_press, smith_machine_incline_press, smith_machine_jm_press, smith_machine_lunge, smith_machine_rdl, smith_machine_seated_press, smith_machine_squat, split_squat_smith.

Note: 9 ids, 8 distinct movements. `ffe_smith_machine_lunge` and `smith_machine_lunge` look like the same movement, the ffe_ one being the front-foot-elevated variant. No separate machine variant is needed; the difference is a platform under the front foot, not the machine.

## Motion
- Bar assembly: prismatic along the guide rods, 7 degrees off vertical (HSSMV: 0 degrees). Travel length not published.
- Hooks: revolute about the bar's long axis; rotation range not sourced (open question).
- Load: plates on the bar-end horns (8 horns), no stack. Bar is counterbalanced on some makes; Hammer Strength lists 30 lb starting resistance.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Placard text not read. Generic rules, to be confirmed at Hukka: set safety stops just below the bottom of the movement; rotate hooks wrist-wise to rack; feet slightly forward of the bar for the squat on an angled model; bench placed so the bar lowers to the lower chest.
- For lunge and split squat, front foot under the bar line; ffe_ variant has the front foot on a plate or step.
