# Hip thrust machine (`hip_thrust_machine`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated on the floor, upper back against two pads, a waist belt/pad loaded by a lever arm; feet on a fixed angled platform; drive hips up to full extension. Serves `machine_hip_thrust`.

## Based on
- Brand/model: Hammer Strength Plate-Loaded Glute Drive (PL-GLD).
- Product pages: https://lifefitness.com/en-us/catalog/strength-training/plate-loaded/hammer-strength-plate-loaded-glute-drive , https://www.lifefitness.com.au/commercial/glute-drive
- Spec sheet / manual: none linked on either page (the only PDF on the US page is an unrelated Pendulum-X infographic).
- Photos: no product image URL was exposed by the fetched pages; the US page has a 3D configurator/AR view. Side-on, front and pivot close-ups need the owner's photos or the configurator. Reseller pages seen in search: https://www.buyandsellfitness.com/products/hammer-strength-plate-loaded-glute-drive , https://threesixtysports.squarespace.com/products/p/preowned-hammer-strength-plate-loaded-glute-drive (images not opened).
- Chosen: Hoist and Titan publish no usable spec for a hip thrust lever (Titan's standing hip thrust page returned HTTP 429). Hammer Strength is a preferred brand with a real spec page.
- Other makers seen for comparison only: TKO 64.8 x 49.4 x 36.7 in https://gymstore.com/tko-signature-plate-loaded-hip-thruster

## Motion
- Single work arm carrying back pads and belt, revolute about a transverse (side-to-side) axis near the floor behind the user (axis location not published, estimated from the machine type).
- ROM not published. Loads plate horns directly (no stack), 4 horns.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- "Zero-adjustment design": no seat, pad or platform adjustment (page).
- Dual-loop waist strap gives a higher lift-off point; half-round back pads leave a spinal relief gap.
- Heels sit on the 15 degree Line-X platform.
