# Seated dip machine (`dip_machine`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated triceps and chest dip: the user sits upright (or slightly reclined), holds two handles beside the hips and pushes them down; the handles are lever arms loaded by a weight stack.
- `seated_dip_machine`.

## Motion
- Two handle arms, each a lever pivoting at the machine's side, moving down and slightly back. Pivot location is not published.
- Fitshop's text says the ROX technology tilts the whole body backwards during the exercise; a dealer description, not checked against the manual, and not a measured angle.
- The arms load the one selectorized stack (100 kg) through cable and pulleys. The weight cage can be installed on the left or right side (assembly manual page 4).
- Range of motion: not published, in degrees or mm.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Seat: ratcheting height adjustment (360 Sports listing), "suitable for any body height and build" (fitshop).
- Handles: adjustable for shoulder width (fitshop) and set the user's position, "meant to reduce shoulder stress" (360 Sports).
- Anchor to the floor: the assembly manual warns the unit can tip.
- Placard rules not found.
