# Power rack (`power_rack`)

## Movement and exercises served
A power rack with a fixed multi-grip pull-up bar and a pair of dip handles bolted on as add-ons. (The issue called this item the pull-up and dip station; it is the rack, since squats will use the same frame.) The mannequin hangs from the bar or supports itself on the dip handles; nothing on the station moves. Serves `pullup`, `pullup_wide_grip`, `close_grip_pullup`, `neutral_grip_pullup`, `2_grip_pullup`, `chinup`, `chinup_wide_grip`, `close_grip_chinup`, `dead_hang`, `hanging_leg_raise`, `dip`, `close_grip_dip`, and the weighted versions `weighted_pullup`, `weighted_chinup`, `2_grip_pullup_weighted`, `weighted_dip`. Not this item: `bent_knee_dragon_flag`, `dragon_flag`, `lying_leg_raise` use a bench.

## Motion
Nothing moves. Hanging: the hands wrap the bar, body hangs below it, so the shoulder joint sits directly beneath the bar; pull-up end position is chin over bar; dead hang is arms straight, elbows locked. Dips: the hands rest on the two handles with arms vertical, torso upright, the lifter's back to the upright, facing out along the handles (close-grip dip video 0:05 and 0:06, the one video on rack-mounted handles), the legs hanging almost straight there and bent behind on the dip tower (dip video 5:06). The dip handles are angled for width and fixed during use.

Range of motion of the lifts (the station is static; this is the body's):
- Pull-up: the body rises about 490 mm, from arms straight to chin over the bar (video 20:06 and 20:08, scaled by the hanging lifter, bar to sole about 2.2 m; rough). Elbows from straight to about 40 degrees.
- Dip: the shoulders drop about 290 mm, from arms locked to the shoulder about level with the elbow, elbows at about 90 degrees (close-grip dip video 0:06 and 0:05, scaled by the lifter, taken as a person of about 1.78 m; rough). The dip video (5:06 to 5:11) shows the same depth with a forward torso lean of about 30 degrees.
- Hanging leg raise: hip from straight to about 90 to 110 degrees of flexion, legs at or above horizontal (video 4:06 and 4:33).

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Pull-up/chin-up variants differ only by hand position: close and neutral on the 406 mm prongs or between them on the cross tube, wide on the cross tube outside the 813 mm prongs or its bent ends, `2_grip_pullup` is a clip a grip, as the library's note and the 2-grip pulldown have it: set 1 overhand on the standard grips (610 mm, about 1.5 shoulder widths), set 2 underhand on the V (280 mm, the nearest the bar has to shoulder width).
- Dip handle spacing is a fixed choice per rig (451 to 629 mm); close-grip dip uses the inner end. Built, the dip's grips are 540 mm apart in the handles' middle and the close grips 487, as near the crosshead as the hips clear it, the shoulders starting a little ahead of the hands.
- The V's arms run about 49 degrees off square, which suits an underhand hand and turns an overhand one palm out, so `close_grip_pullup` holds the cross tube hard by the inner prongs, 544 mm apart, the nearest an overhand grip gets on this bar.
- Where a hanging body is: the dip handles are on the +x upright, so a body on the bar hangs beside them, not between them, at least 70 mm clear.
- Bar height: the hanging lifter's feet just clear the floor with the legs straight (pull-up 20:06, dead hang 0:18). For the mannequin (standing shoulder 1.45 m, arm to grip 0.63 m, about 0.05 m of shoulder lift when hanging), a grip at 2.30 m leaves its soles about 0.17 m and its pointed toes about 0.07 m off the floor.
- Dip height: with the arms locked the lifter's feet hang well clear; at the bottom of the dip they come near the floor or a box, and the lifters bend the knees (close-grip dip 0:05, dip 5:06). For the mannequin, handles at 1.30 m put its shoulders at about 1.90 m locked out and its soles about 0.45 m up, 0.16 m up at the bottom.
- Weight belt, as the videos show: weighted pull-ups and dips use a dip belt with a chain; the plate hangs on the chain in front of the thighs, between the legs at about mid-thigh, not at the hips (pull-up 20:06, weighted dip 1:30). The mannequin shows a plate hanging there.

## Open gaps
- The rack's height is 9 ft, not the RML-390BT's 90 3/8 in: no published 90 in rack puts a crossmember on a hole high enough for the bar height below. Whether a 9 ft Monster Lite power rack is sold is not confirmed.
- The end plates' height and bolt spacing, the pipe safeties' height and the Matador bracket's wrap are read from photos.
- Bar and dip handle heights depend on where the gym mounted them on its rig; no maker publishes them. The bar height is chosen from the fit rule above (feet just clear, as the videos show), and the dip height is a rough estimate from one video, scaled by a person of about 1.78 m. Both low confidence.
- The Crown numbers not on the page (cross tube offset, prong lengths, wide-grip spacing) are scaled from one top-down photo by its own labelled 32 in dimension; good to about +-20 mm.
- The chin-up video is unavailable, so the chin-up's hand position is taken from the pull-up (same bar, supinated grip).
- Whether the owner's gym has a rig with these parts, a power tower, or a Hammer Strength or Hoist unit is unknown and stays so. The Rogue parts stand in for the type.
