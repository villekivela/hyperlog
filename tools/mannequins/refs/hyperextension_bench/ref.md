# 45-degree hyperextension bench (`hyperextension_bench`)

## Movement and exercises served
A frame with an angled hip pad and a heel/ankle support: the lifter lies face-down over the pad and raises or lowers the torso at the hip. Serves `45_hyperextension` and `roman_chair_crunch` (done facing up on the same frame). No part of the bench moves while in use.

## Motion
The frame is rigid; only the setup is adjustable. The lifter pivots at the hip over the pad (a revolute joint at the hip, axis side to side) from about 45 degrees (hips on pad, torso down below the line) up to a straight line from head to heel. Feet are held in the heel pad with the legs inline with the torso at the top.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Pad angle 35 to 50 degrees (use 45 for the animation).
- The hip pad slides along the slope (844-1119 mm range) so the pad top sits just below the pelvis (hip crease) so the torso can hinge freely.
- The heel lock holds the heels; the thighs rest on the leg pad.
- Roman chair crunch: the lifter faces up on the same frame, hips on the pad, ankles hooked in the heel lock.
- Nordic hamstring curl and reverse nordic: bodyweight, no separate machine. Nordic uses an ankle anchor (a partner, a loaded barbell or a pad on the floor at the knees); reverse nordic uses a kneeling pad with the feet anchored. A pad and an anchor point as a small note, not a separate item.
