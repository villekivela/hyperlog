# Cable crossover (`cable_crossover`)

Free-standing dual adjustable-pulley machine: two towers, each with its own stack and a pulley carriage that slides to any of 16 heights. Not the single-column `cable_column` kit.

## Movement and exercises served
The user stands (or sits/kneels/lies) between or beside the towers and pulls a handle on a cable. Each side is independent: one-arm or two-arm, pull direction set by carriage height (low = curls, kickbacks, low-to-high press; mid = press, flye, row-like face pull; high = crossover, triceps pushdown/extension, high lateral raise). Cable axis is free in the swivel (180 degrees).

## Motion
- Pulley carriage (each tower): prismatic, vertical, 16 detent positions, adjusted by pin; set before the set, static during it.
- Pulley swivel: revolute about the carriage's rail-normal axis, 180 degrees (page: "180-degree swivel pulley design"). Lets the cable leave at any angle in that arc.
- Cable and handle: free end, 2743 mm of handle travel; stack moves half that (2:1 ratio).
- Stack (each side): prismatic, vertical, 250 lb in 10 lb steps, giving up to 125 lb at the handle (CT-1 page). Left and right stacks are independent.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Placard text not available. Rules below are generic and must be confirmed at Hukka.
- Pick carriage height from the exercise: roughly knee/ankle for kickbacks and curls, shoulder to chest for flye and press, top for crossover and high lateral raise.
- Same height on both towers for two-arm lifts.
- Stand far enough in front of the column line that the cable is taut at the start.
- Attachments: carabiner handle, rope, strap, ankle cuff (included: leather tricep straps and carabiners per page).
