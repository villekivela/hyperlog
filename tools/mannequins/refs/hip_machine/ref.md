# Hip abduction / adduction machine (`hip_machine`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated, back supported, thighs pressed against two leg pads (or knee pads) that swing about vertical axes near the hip joints. Abduction pushes the knees apart; adduction squeezes them together. Serves `machine_hip_abduction` and `machine_adduction`.

**One machine, no variant.** The kinematics are identical (two arms, each a revolute about a vertical axis, same stack); only the start angle and the direction of travel differ. Hammer Strength sells these as two cabinets (HS-HAB, HS-HAD), and Hoist as two (RS-1407 outer, RS-1406 inner), but the movement is one mechanism with a different start position. Model a start-angle parameter, not two machines. Verify at Hukka whether the gym has a combined unit (see photo list).

## Motion
- Two leg arms, one per leg, each revolute about a vertical axis (the placard's red axis-of-rotation indicator sits at the hip).
- Start angle set by a ratchet in 10 degree steps (HS-HAB page). Total ROM per leg not published (estimated about 40 to 60 degrees from typical use, unverified).
- Both arms are linked to one stack by cable; stack travel not published.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Seat adjusts with a roller mechanism; range not published.
- Start position ratchet, 10 degree increments; abduction starts knees together, adduction starts knees apart.
- Placard rule (from the page): align the red axis marker with the hip joint. Kneepads and two foot positions support the legs.
