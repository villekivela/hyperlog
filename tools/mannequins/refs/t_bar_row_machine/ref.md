# Chest-supported T-bar row machine (`t_bar_row_machine`)

Plate-loaded lever with a low front pivot. Not the seated `row_machine` (laid out as a Gymleco 010, a sit-down row with a vertical chest pad): here the user stands on a foot plate and lies prone on an inclined chest pad.

## Movement and exercises served
The user stands on the foot plate, leans onto the inclined chest pad, and pulls the handles toward the chest. The handles sit on a lever that pivots low at the front; plates go on a horn at the rear end of the lever. Chest support takes the lower back out of the lift. The same setup serves the shrug: elevate the shoulders against the chest pad with the arms straight.

## Motion
- One lever pivoting low at the front, about 250 mm above the floor (chosen, low confidence; see Open gaps). The handles sit on the lever ahead of the user; the plate horn is at the rear, behind the user's feet.
- The handles travel on an arc up and back toward the chest: a fixed arc, not a free bar.
- Axis: horizontal, across the machine, at the front.
- Range of motion: not published. Chosen 280 mm of handle travel along the arc, low confidence.
- The horn is a 50 mm sleeve for Olympic plates, 370 mm long (Titan page).

## Handles
- Neutral: vertical grips, 230 mm long, 445 mm apart.
- Wide: horizontal grips, 140 mm long, 660 mm apart.
- Both are fixed on the Titan lever; 31 mm rubber.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Foot plate: pop-pin adjustable position; the title says "adjustable" and the page names no other adjustment, so the chest pad is assumed fixed (not confirmed).
- Fit: 4 ft 11 in to 6 ft 6 in (Titan), so the placard rule is a height range. Chest on the pad, handles reachable with arms straight, feet flat on the plate.
- Placard rules: not found.
