# Pull-up and dip station (`pullup_dip_station`)

## Movement and exercises served
A fixed overhead multi-grip bar plus a pair of dip handles, on one rig. The mannequin hangs from the bar or supports itself on the dip handles; nothing on the station moves. Serves `pullup`, `pullup_wide_grip`, `close_grip_pullup`, `neutral_grip_pullup`, `2_grip_pullup`, `chinup`, `chinup_wide_grip`, `close_grip_chinup`, `dead_hang`, `hanging_leg_raise`, `dip`, `close_grip_dip`, and the weighted versions `weighted_pullup`, `weighted_chinup`, `2_grip_pullup_weighted`, `weighted_dip`. Not this item: `bent_knee_dragon_flag`, `dragon_flag`, `lying_leg_raise` use a bench.

## Motion
Nothing moves. Hanging: the hands wrap the bar, body hangs below it, so the shoulder joint sits directly beneath the bar; pull-up end position is chin over bar; dead hang is arms straight, elbows locked. Dips: the hands rest on the two handles with arms vertical, torso upright, legs behind. The dip handles are angled for width and fixed during use.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Pull-up/chin-up variants differ only by hand position: close and neutral on the 406 mm spheres or inner grips, wide on the 813 mm spheres or the bar ends, `2_grip_pullup` mixes one overhand and one neutral.
- Dip handle spacing is a fixed choice per rig (451 to 629 mm); close-grip dip uses the inner position.
- The bar height is set so the lifter's feet clear the floor on dead hang; the mannequin should hang with arms fully extended and feet at least about 100 mm off the floor (my assumption).
- Weight belt: weighted pull-up, chin-up, dip load a belt or chain at the hips; no belt product was verified here, so the mannequin should only show a plate hanging from the hips (a plate disc, see the README note).
