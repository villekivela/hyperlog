# Lateral raise machine (`lateral_raise_machine`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated shoulder abduction: the user's forearms or hands press against pads on two lever arms whose pivots line up with the shoulder joints, and the arms rise out to the sides, loaded by one weight stack.
- `machine_lateral_raise`: seated.
- `standing_machine_lateral_raise`: the same movement. No source shows a separate standing machine. Treat it as this machine with the user standing beside the seat (seat dropped or ignored), or as the same kit with a standing pose. Whether a standing user can actually reach the pads on the Hukka machine is unverified; see the last section.

## Motion
- Left arm and right arm: each revolute about a horizontal axis pointing front to back through the shoulder (the page marks "axis of rotation" with a red indicator). Arms rise from the sides to roughly horizontal.
- Range of motion: not published. Do not assume 90 degrees; measure at Hukka.
- Both arms load the one 95 kg selectorized stack through cable and pulleys (pulleys 11 cm and 15 cm, per the page).
- Handles pivot to fit users of all sizes (page text), so there is a second small revolute at each hand grip.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Seat height by roller mechanism; the placard rule is to align the shoulder joint with the red axis indicator (from the page).
- Pad positions are optimised for the middle deltoid (fitshop page).
- Pivoting handles accommodate different user sizes.

## Owner photos to take at Hukka
- Side-on and front-on, same distance, with a person or tape for scale.
- Close-up of the arm pivot and its red indicator, and the seat at lowest and highest with floor-to-seat measurement.
- Arms at rest and at the top of the stroke, side-on, to read the range in degrees.
- Whether the machine can be used standing (arm pads reachable from the floor) or only seated.
- The placard.
