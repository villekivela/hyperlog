# Seated crunch machine (`crunch_machine`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated, feet held on supports, pad or handles at the chest or shoulders; flex the spine by curling the torso forward against a counterbalanced lever on a stack. Serves `machine_crunch`.

## Motion
- One work arm (the "AbCam" system), revolute about a transverse axis in front of or at the chest; the cam mimics the natural crunch so resistance varies. Axis location and ROM not published.
- Counterbalanced arm gives low starting resistance; cable to the 95 kg stack.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Seat adjusts via a roller mechanism (range not published). Two foot supports need no adjustment.
- Placard marks the axis of rotation with a red indicator: align it with the user's waist or lower spine (inferred from the marker, placement not stated).
