# Shoulder press machine (`shoulder_press`)

## Movement and exercises served
Seated overhead press on a lever yoke loaded by one weight stack. The yoke pivots behind the back pad at about shoulder height; its two side arms run forward past the user's shoulders to the grips. Hands start level with the shoulders and push up and a little back.
- `machine_shoulder_press`.

One machine: the bar grip versus the neutral grip is a hand-position choice, not a different movement.

## Motion
- One yoke: both side arms are joined by the crossbar behind the pad (hero photo), so the two grips move together. The page does not call it iso-lateral, and the demo shows both grips rising at once.
- Revolute about one horizontal axis across the machine, behind the back pad at about grip height. The grips travel up and a little back on an arc of about 900 mm radius.
- Range of motion: the grips rise about 580 mm, from level with the shoulders (demo 0:08) to the arms near straight overhead (demo 0:11 to 0:12). Estimated from those frames, scaled by the 1630 mm height. With the 900 mm arm that is about 40 degrees of yoke rotation, and the grips end about 200 mm behind where they started; the angle is low confidence, since no view is side-on.
- The seat sets the start: the page says "Seat position provides range-of-motion adjustments". A higher seat puts the shoulders above the grips, so the press starts deeper.
- The yoke loads the 95 kg stack through cable and pulleys.

## Handles
- Each side arm has a straight bar end (pronated "bar grip") and a curved grip beside it (neutral grip), per the page and the hero photo. The demo uses the curved grips.

## Adjustments and fit rules
- Seat height, by a lever under the seat front (demo 0:01; page).
- Fit rule, checked against the demo at 0:08: the grips are level with the shoulders at the bottom of the rep, with the elbows below the hands. That was the rule to confirm; the demo shows it.
- Red indicator: the page says it marks the axis of rotation. The axis is behind the pad at about shoulder height, so lining the shoulder up with it means setting the seat so the shoulders are at the grips' height. The demo's user sits that way.

## Open gaps
- Pivot place, yoke length and the rotation angle: no side-on photo or frame was found, so they come from oblique views and are low confidence.
- Seat range: no frame shows the seat at its ends; chosen.
- For the mannequin (seated shoulder about 0.63 m above the seat top): with the seat at 0.47 m the shoulders sit at 1.10 m, 120 mm above the estimated grips. Chosen from the fit rule: either lower the seat to about 0.35 m (as the demo's user sits), or set the grips and pivot at 1.10 m. Low confidence either way.
